# SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator 0.1.0

The first release: sign in once on each of your devices, and everything of yours is signed in
and in step.

- **One sign-in for everything.** Sign in on your laptop or phone (Authentik, or a password) and
  SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic, Health, Video, Maps and Email on that device open already signed in,
  at your own servers. Each of them needs its release that knows the orchestrator, and its
  address entered on its Admin page.
- **Notifications everywhere.** What pings one device shows on the others; dismiss it once.
- **Clipboard and links** to any of your devices, from the phone's share sheet, the
  `sam-orchestrator` command, or the web page.
- **Carry on where you were**: what's playing or open on one device is offered on the others.
- **Ring** a device to find it.
- **Sealed.** What your devices send each other is sealed for the receiving device before it
  leaves; the server passes it on and can't read it.
- **Your devices** on one page: connected or not, batteries, rename, and remove (which signs a
  lost device out).
- The server with its web page, the Linux agent (`.deb`, `.rpm`, and in SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux),
  and the Android app, in the family's look.
